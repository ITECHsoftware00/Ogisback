Privacy policy

OgisBack is a marketplace where creators share work and clients arrange collaborations. This policy explains how the service handles information when you visit, sign in or use a workspace.

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we use the account identifier and the profile information the provider shares, such as your name, email address and profile picture, to identify you, create your account and display your profile. Your provider handles its own sign-in screen. Supabase processes authentication and stores account and marketplace data. The browser stores session information to keep you signed in.

Profiles, posts and collaborations

We store information you add to your profile, such as your bio, location, social handles, niches, portfolio media and rates. Public profiles, published posts, comments, reviews and open campaign briefs can be viewed by other people. Messages, pitches, orders and attachments are processed to deliver them to the people involved and operate the collaboration.

Connected social accounts

Signing in and connecting a channel for analytics are separate actions. If you connect a supported social account, OgisBack uses the permissions you grant to retrieve profile details, audience statistics and follower or subscriber counts. We use connection tokens to retrieve and refresh those details. Creator metrics may appear on your profile or in information shown to prospective clients. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. Contact us to request deletion of previously imported data.

Service providers and payments

Our hosting and database providers process information to serve pages, store content, deliver requests and troubleshoot errors. When you use a Stripe payment or billing feature, Stripe processes your payment details. OgisBack uses transaction references, amounts and payment status to manage orders, wallet activity and billing.

Your information and choices

You can edit your profile and manage your posts in your workspace. For access, correction, deletion or a question about retention, contact us using the email associated with your account and tell us what information the request concerns. We may need to verify account ownership. Some records may need to be retained for outstanding transactions, disputes or legal obligations.
